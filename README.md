GoldMint is a World of Warcraft addon built to help keep track of the things that matter when you're playing—gold, farming, professions, world quests, weekly tasks, cooldowns, and other useful stuff.

The idea is pretty simple: open GoldMint and see what needs your attention without having a bunch of separate addons doing the same thing.

What GoldMint Does
Dashboard

The main dashboard gives you a quick look at your current character and the things GoldMint thinks are worth checking.

It can show things like:

Current gold
Farming information
Current tasks
Next suggestions
Profession information
World quests
Weekly activities
Cooldowns
Other tracked activities
Gold & Farming

GoldMint keeps track of your farming sessions and can show your gold-per-hour information.

It can also keep a recent list of loot collected during a farming session so you can see where your gold is coming from.

Low-value loot can be filtered out so it doesn't clutter the display.

World Quests

GoldMint can keep an eye on World Quests that are actually worth doing.

The World Quests section looks for quests that meet the configured gold requirement, as well as quests involving things such as mounts and achievements.

GoldMint is also designed to avoid constantly rescanning the map or repeatedly alerting you about the same quest.

Weekly Tasks

The Weekly Task Overview puts weekly activities in one place so you don't have to remember everything yourself.

GoldMint can track tasks as you complete them and remove them when they're finished.

Professions

Profession information is only checked when it is actually needed.

GoldMint doesn't need to sit in the background checking whether the Profession window is open every few seconds. When you open the profession interface, GoldMint can update the information it needs.

Cooldowns

Cooldown tracking is intentionally lightweight.

GoldMint isn't constantly scanning your character every few seconds just to find out whether something changed. Cooldown information is updated when appropriate instead.

Reminders

GoldMint includes optional reminders such as Breathe, Stretch and Hydrate.

These reminders are completely optional.

If you don't have a reminder enabled, GoldMint doesn't sit there checking for one. There's no reason to spend CPU time checking something that isn't being used.

Alerts

GoldMint can notify you when something useful happens, including things such as:

A new World Quest that matches your settings
Valuable BoE items
Unknown transmogs
Cooldowns becoming available
Next suggestions
Other configured alerts

Alerts can be adjusted or muted through the GoldMint options.

Performance

Performance is a big part of GoldMint's development.

A lot of the addon is event-driven rather than constantly running timers. The goal is to have GoldMint do work when something actually changes, rather than repeatedly checking the same information in the background.

This is especially important during:

Dungeons
World Quests
Farming
Combat
Opening and closing bags

If a feature isn't being used, GoldMint tries not to spend CPU time checking it.

Slash Commands

The main command is:

/gm

This opens the GoldMint interface.

Additional commands may be available depending on the current version.

Current Status

GoldMint is currently in Beta.

That means things are still being worked on and changed. Some features may be adjusted as World of Warcraft changes or as performance issues are found.

If something isn't working correctly, an error message or screenshot is usually very helpful when reporting it.
